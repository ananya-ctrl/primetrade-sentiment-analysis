Key Findings

1. Fear days showed the strongest performance in the aligned sample, with the highest average total daily PnL, highest average PnL per trade, and highest win rate.

2. Fear days also showed the highest trading activity and the largest average trade size.

3. Greed days appeared relatively more sell-heavy, with a buy/sell ratio below 1.

4. Frequent traders outperformed infrequent traders on both average daily PnL and win rate.

5. High Size traders had slightly higher average daily PnL than Low Size traders, but their average win rate was lower.

Important Note:
The overlap between the sentiment dataset and historical trader dataset is limited, so these findings should be treated as directional insights based on the aligned sample.
